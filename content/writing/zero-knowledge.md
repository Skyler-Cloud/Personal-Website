A zero-knowledge proof is a protocol in which a verifier can be convinced that a statement is true without learning additional information beyond its truth (under the relevant security definition).

## A relation

Consider the relation

$$R = \{(x,w): f(w)=x\}.$$

A proof of knowledge for this relation concerns knowledge of a witness $w$ satisfying the relation. Zero knowledge is a separate property.
